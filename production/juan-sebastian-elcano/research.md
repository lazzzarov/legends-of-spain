## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **Crew at departure.** The sources give 234 (the 1518 agreement, in Stanley's appendix), about 230 (a contemporary report in Stanley), 265 or 270 (the historians cited by RAH) and 285 (Real Instituto Elcano). The fact shows "234–285" with `status: disputed`.
- **Date of death.** RAH gives 6 August 1526. The dossier and the earlier draft gave 4 August. The file now records `August 1526` with no day, and the `more` text notes the disagreement.
- **Men detained at Cape Verde.** RAH says 12 and Pigafetta says 13. The text says "about a dozen".
- **Distance and duration.** RAH gives 46,270 nautical miles (85,700 km) over 1,084 days; Pigafetta reckons "more than 14,460 leagues". Neither is used as a headline fact because the two are hard to reconcile. Either could be added, attributed to its source.
- **The letter.** It survives in the AGI as a simple copy alongside an anonymous opinion paper. The text calls it "a copy" in `more` and never claims it is the original.

## Open questions

- The document images for PATRONATO,48,R.20 and PATRONATO,38,R.1 have not been checked. They should be, to confirm the wording and date of the will.
- The surviving Asian men on the Victoria: how many reached Spain, their names and what became of them. Herrera mentions some; a modern study is needed.
- The 6 August against 4 August death date: check Urdaneta's account of the Loaísa voyage and the testament papers.
- `production/juan-sebastian-elcano/comic-script.md` and `video-brief.md` were written against the old draft. Check them against the revised panels and dates (death in August 1526, Tidore decision, Cape Verde). They were not edited, which is outside this task.
