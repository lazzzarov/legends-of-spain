- **Status:** draft, awaiting the editor's approval. No footage is generated before approval.
- **Length:** 60 s target (45–90 s allowed) · 16:9 master, 9:16 social cut.
- **Voice:** English, third person, one narrator for the whole series. No invented dialogue.

- **Disclosure (required):** "AI-generated historical dramatization" on screen for the first 3 s and on the end card.

## Hook

A garrison left behind on a hostile coast is offered safe passage and money to leave. Its captains decide together to refuse, and no help is coming.

## Character sheet

Use the same description in every prompt.

- **Sarmiento:** Castilian maestre de campo from Burgos, aged about forty-one (born c. 1498). A veteran of some twenty years of campaigns. Dark hair and beard going grey. Dark doublet and hose of the late 1530s, a plain breastplate and an open helmet, sword. Dusty and worn by the siege; wounded on the last day. His rank, origin and approximate age are documented; his face, build and exact dress are interpretation.
- **Likeness:** no verified likeness exists. No portrait, contemporary or later, was found (see the `media` note in the legend file). Keep his face in shadow or turned away where possible. The result is always labelled an AI interpretation and is never used as a portrait.
- **The garrison:** Spanish infantry of the 1530s, plus a few light horsemen (`rah-sarmiento`). Ragged after a winter of digging. The captains are shown deciding together.
- **Non-combatants:** women, children and merchants were in the town and sheltered in the lower castle (`floristan-2023`, `gonzalez-castrillo-2013`). They appear in the town scenes.
- **The Ottoman force:** shown at a distance as a disciplined army and fleet, without caricature. Check janissary dress against a source.
- **Alcaraz and Tapia:** two corporals, ordinary soldiers, whose sworn statement of 4 September 1539 is the best evidence for the siege (`alcaraz-tapia-1539`).

## Do not show

- The killing of the wounded, the enslavement of the people in the lower castle or the burning of the town. They are part of the story and are never shown graphically.
- Sarmiento's death or his body; it was never identified.
- Sarmiento alone speaking a defiant reply; the legend file marks that line as legend. The answer was a council's.
- Every defender dying; the legend file marks this as legend. Hundreds were taken captive.
- A relief fleet on the horizon; none was planned.
- Any figure for the size of the Ottoman force on screen.
- Anachronisms: comb morions, wide-brimmed hats and dress of the later tercios, uniforms, the red-and-yellow Spanish flag, later sailing warships, star-shaped bastions.

## Numbers allowed in voiceover

- 23 July 1539, the offer refused; twenty ducats a man (`alcaraz-tapia-1539`, `floristan-2023`).
- A fortnight of bombardment; by 7 August 1539 the defences had given way (`alcaraz-tapia-1539`, `rah-sarmiento`). No exact date of death: sources give 6 or 7 August.
- Hundreds taken captive, with no figure: sources range from 200 to 800.
- No figure for the garrison (2,500–4,000) or the Ottoman force (22,000–150,000), and no figure for Ottoman losses.
