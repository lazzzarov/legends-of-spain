## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **Origin of the 22 children.** Tuells & Duro-Torrijos (table naming each child) give the Hospital de la Caridad in A Coruña and the Santiago foundling home. RAH says the Coruña foundling home only, and Asensi Botet says 4 from Madrid and 18 from Santiago. The file follows Tuells, the most detailed source: "institutions in A Coruña and Santiago de Compostela".
- **Zendal's name.** The forms Zendal / Sendales / Cendala / Celdán all appear (RAH uses "Isabel Sendales Gómez"; Tuells's table prints "Zendeja" and the text "Zendal Gómez"). The file uses the now-standard "Isabel Zendal".
- **Enslaved carriers in Havana.** RAH and Tuells 2015 say three enslaved women were bought. Tuells & Franco-Paredes (2023) quote an AGI record as "we bought four slaves" and argue that enslaved people were seldom used. The file gives both numbers in `otherSide`.
- **Acapulco departure date.** 7 Feb (Tuells, after Díaz de Yraola) or 8 Feb 1805 (RAH). The file avoids the exact day.
- **Pacific ship.** RAH and Tuells name the _Magallanes_. Asensi names the _Santa Bárbara_, probably a misreading of "santabárbara", the ship's powder magazine, which Balmis mentions. The file gives no ship name.
- **End date of the expedition.** It is variously given as 1806 (Balmis's return), 1810 (Salvany's death) or 1813 (Tuells & Franco-Paredes, counting the second Mexican trip and the work in South America). The file uses 1803–1806 for Balmis's own voyage and states the later dates explicitly.
- **"Millions saved".** Asensi writes "miles, tal vez millones". No total figure was verified, so none is used.

## Open questions

- The claim, repeated by the Spanish Embassy and many others, that the WHO recognised Isabel Zendal in 1950 as the first nurse on an international mission. No WHO document was found, so the claim is left out of the file. It could become a `myth` once checked.
- Zendal's later life and death (reportedly Puebla, Mexico). Unverified.
- The Supplement to the _Gazeta de Madrid_, 14 Oct 1806 (copy at Wellcome Collection; studied in Tuells et al., Rev Esp Salud Pública 2023;97:e202310083) was not consulted. It would be a good primary source for the legacy chapter.
- AGI file "1558" (cited by Tuells & Franco-Paredes) and AGN México, Epidemias, vol. 19, box 5881 (Tuells 2015) were not consulted directly. PARES catalogue entries should be added once checked.
- The exact Lisbon arrival date: RAH gives 14 Aug 1806, and some secondary sources give 15 Aug.
