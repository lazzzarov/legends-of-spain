## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **Size of the Indigenous coalition.** RAH (from Spanish accounts) gives ~25,000 allies at Texcoco in May 1521; Navarrete (via Paz Nomey) estimates 100,000–200,000 across the war. Given as a range, `status: disputed`.
- **Cholula death toll.** RAH gives 3,000 (Cortés's own figure). The YAML says "thousands" and does not give a number.
- **Smallpox deaths (5–8 million).** A single modern estimate; labelled `disputed`, with a note that all population figures for the period are reconstructions.
- **Cortés as Quetzalcoatl.** Dupey argues the sources do suggest the identification and that _teotl_ is ambiguous; the sceptical view (Restall, León-Portilla citing Frankl) was found only in search snippets and not opened. Labelled `disputed`, not `legend`.
- **Date of Tenochtitlan's fall.** RAH: Cuauhtémoc surrendered 12 Aug; fighting 30 May–13 Aug. The conventional 13 August 1521 is used.
- **Tone of RAH.** The entry praises Cortés's "genius" and calls Tlaxcalan fidelity a "gift of destiny". Its judgements are not reproduced; Navarrete's coalition reading is given equal weight in the chapters.

## Left out (could not verify from an opened source)

- The Veracruz _cabildo_ as a legal device to escape Velázquez's authority (dossier claim; not in the sources read).
- Tlaxcalan petitions to the Crown citing their alliance (dossier claim).
- The year of Cuauhtémoc's execution (1525 is conventional; RAH gives no year).
- Burial place(s) of Cortés.
