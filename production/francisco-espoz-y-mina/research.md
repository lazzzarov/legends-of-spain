## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **Size of his force and the casualties he inflicted.** These figures come from his own memoirs and service record. The file presents them as ranges and claims with `status: disputed` and mentions the 1817 Navarrese returns.
- **Execution of Cabrera's mother (1836).** The RAH describes it as a Cristino reprisal without naming who ordered it, while the Gran Enciclopèdia Catalana says Espoz y Mina had her executed. The file says he "was held responsible", and his personal role should be checked against a dedicated study.
- **His surname.** Portillo's review says Esdaile treats the name "Mina" as usurped (borrowed from the nephew's fame), while the RAH gives his father's name as Juan Esteban Espoz y Mina. This is not used in the file and is left as an open question.
- **Motives for 1814.** The RAH biographer calls the rising improvised and doubts its constitutional motive, while Espoz's memoirs justify it as a constitutional act. The file reports the event and the RAH's scepticism.
- **Castellfollit / Castellfullit.** The RAH writes "Castellfullit", and the Enciclopèdia Catalana specifies Castellfollit de Riubregós. The file uses "Castellfollit" without the qualifier.

## Open questions

- Esdaile's _España contra Napoleón_ (2006) and _Fighting Napoleon_ (2004), and John Tone's _The Fatal Knot_ (Spanish edition _La guerrilla española y la derrota de Napoleón_), were not consulted directly. They should be read for coercive recruitment and requisitioning in Navarre specifically.
- No Archivo Real y General de Navarra catalogue entries were opened. Candidates include Espoz's administrative orders and customs receipts.
- The _Memorias_ (1851–52) and the 1825 London _Breve extracto de la vida del General Mina_ (a digitised copy is on Commons and Internet Archive) were not read.
- French reports on the war in Navarre, and Reille's and Bessières's decrees in the original, were not read.
- The 1830 Vera attempt: the RAH text says 20 Oct, while its event timeline says 14 Oct (Valdés's entry). Not used in the file.
