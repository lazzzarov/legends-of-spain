# Video script — Blas de Lezo: "The City That Would Not Fall"

- **Status:** draft, awaiting the editor's approval. No footage is generated before approval.
- **Length:** 60 s target (45–90 s allowed) · 16:9 master, 9:16 social cut.
- **Voice:** English, third person, one narrator for the whole series. No invented dialogue.
- **Look:** painted chronicle (animated oil painting and engraving, not photoreal). See `docs/PLAN_V2.md`.
- **Disclosure (required):** "AI-generated historical dramatization" on screen for the first 3 s and on the end card.

## Hook

A huge fleet appears off a fortress city defended by six warships and a few thousand men. Its naval commander and its viceroy quarrel throughout. Together they have to hold until the attackers break.

## Character sheet

Use the same description in every prompt.

- **Lezo:** Basque naval officer, lieutenant general, aged 52 at the siege (born February 1689). Left leg lost below the knee, so a wooden leg; left eye sightless; right arm in place but useless, held close to the body. He did not lose the arm. Spanish naval officer's dress of about 1740: long dark blue coat with red cuffs and gold lace, white wig, tricorn. Check the coat against the Museo Naval portrait before the first still is approved.
- **Likeness:** the legend file has a portrait (`portrait.jpg`, an 1853 copy of an original owned by his descendants). The likeness may be guided by it, but the result is an AI interpretation and is always labelled as one.
- **Documented and interpretation:** each wound, his rank and the dates are documented (`rah-lezo`). His expressions, and where he stood at any moment of the siege, are interpretation.
- **Eslava:** viceroy and holder of supreme command; army general's coat, wig. No likeness is given in the legend file, so do not give him a caricatured or villainous face.
- **The garrison:** regular battalions, local militia, companies of pardos and Indigenous troops, and the engineer Carlos Desnaux. Show them in every scene on the walls.
- **The attackers:** British sailors and soldiers, North American colonists and Black machete-men from Jamaica. Shown at a distance, never mocked.

## Do not show

- The dead and dying of either side: no bodies at the foot of San Felipe, no fever wards in close-up.
- Lezo fighting hand to hand, or alone on a wall facing the fleet.
- Lezo with a missing arm, a hook or an eye patch as costume; the arm was disabled, not lost.
- Any caption or image calling this the greatest defeat in British history; the legend file treats that as rhetoric.
- The medals showing Lezo kneeling, unless the editor adds a line for them.
- Anachronisms: the red-and-yellow Spanish flag (later than 1741), the Union flag with the red diagonal cross (later than 1741), uniforms and ships of Nelson's time, steam or iron of any kind.

## Numbers allowed in voiceover

- Six Spanish warships (`rah-lezo`, `rah-eslava`).
- Dates: 15 March, 20 April, 20 May and 7 September 1741 (`rah-lezo`, `rah-eslava`).
- Bocachica held for more than two weeks (`rah-lezo`, `rah-eslava`).
- No single figure for Vernon's fleet or army: sources range from about 115 to 200 vessels and about 24,000 to 31,000 men. Use only the legend file's own phrases: well over a hundred sail, a few thousand defenders.
- No total for British losses: the estimate near 18,000 is disputed.
