## Disputed points and handling

- **Force sizes:** all given as ranges with `status: disputed`. I avoided the RAH Lezo entry's rhetorical ratio ("each Spaniard defeated ten Englishmen") and its "largest fleet ever" framing.
- **"Greatest British naval defeat in history":** treated as rhetoric in a myth. Even the Museo Naval caption calls it "one of the worst naval defeats in English history", which I did not adopt.
- **Credit:** shared explicitly with Eslava, Desnaux, the battalions, the militia, the pardo companies and the Indigenous troops.
- **Cause of death:** the RAH entry attributes it to wounds received in the defence; other accounts mention epidemic disease after the siege. The YAML states only the date and place.
- **Medals "struck prematurely":** supported by two RAH entries. The RMG object confirms that such medals exist and contradict the outcome, but not when they were struck.

## Open questions

- Primary records named in the dossier were **not consulted**: AHN ESTADO,2335,Exp.2 (Lezo's diary and letter); AGI SANTA_FE,1021; Desnaux's relation (Servicio Histórico Militar, sign. 6888); the British _Authentic papers_ (1744). They should be checked in PARES before review.
- The date and place of the eye wound (often given as Toulon, 1707) are not in the RAH entry.
- Pasajes de San Pedro vs San Juan: the RAH label and metadata differ.
- A scholarly British count of the expedition, e.g. Richard Harding's _Amphibious Warfare in the Eighteenth Century_ (1991) or J. de Zulueta's article in _Mariner's Mirror_ (1992), would help firm up the ranges.
