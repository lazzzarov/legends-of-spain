## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **Fleet sizes off São Miguel, 1582.** The RGM gives about 25 against more than 60. The RAH calls the French fleet stronger, with 30 large ships. Shown as "c. 25 vs 30–60+", `disputed`.
- **Who sought battle in 1582.** The RAH says Bazán attacked without waiting for the Andalusian squadron. The RGM says Bazán was waiting for Recalde and Strozzi sought the fight. The file avoids saying "he attacked anyway" and says only that he fought outnumbered and without his reinforcements.
- **1583 dates.** The RAH says the defenders surrendered "after the landing and the first fighting (23 July 1583)". The RGM narrates the landing at 04:55 on 26 July and the entry into Angra on the 27th. The RGM is followed for the landing. The RAH date may refer to another event or be an error. **To verify** against Cerezo Martínez (1983).
- **1583 force.** Only the RGM gives totals (98 ships, more than 8,000 soldiers). Marked `disputed`, with that explanation.
- **Age at Muros.** Born December 1526, so he was 17 in July 1544. The RAH says "only eighteen". The file says "still in his teens".
- **Executions.** The RAH stresses royal instructions. The RGM adds Fernández Duro's claim that Henry III had authorised punishment, which was left out as contested. The myth is marked `disputed`: the order was the king's, but Bazán carried it out.
- **Armada counterfactual.** Treated as a `legend` (speculative). An RGM article (Pinedo del Campo) argues Bazán's plan was better than Farnese's. It was not used, being an argument rather than evidence.
- **Lepanto "decisive" claims.** RGM authors argue that Bazán was decisive. The file describes only what he did and leaves the overall credit to the coalition under Don John.

## Open questions

- Confirm that "playa de las Molas" is today's Baía das Mós (São Sebastião, Terceira). The file says "probably".
- Confirm the date of the 1583 landing (23 or 26 July) in Cerezo Martínez (1983) or in García de Alarcón (1585) and Mosquera de Figueroa (1596).
- A PARES authority record for Bazán (named in the dossier) was not opened.
- Whether the Rijksmuseum print shows the first marquis (see below).
