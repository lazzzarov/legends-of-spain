- **Status:** draft, awaiting the editor's approval. No footage is generated before approval.
- **Length:** 60 s target (45–90 s allowed) · 16:9 master, 9:16 social cut.
- **Voice:** English, third person, one narrator for the whole series. No invented dialogue.
- **Look:** painted chronicle (animated oil painting and engraving, not photoreal). See `docs/PLAN_V2.md`.

## Hook

An army looking for winter quarters is trapped on a drowning island by an enemy who has cut the dykes. Nothing its commander tries works. Then the weather changes.

## Character sheet

Use the same description in every prompt.

- **Bobadilla:** Castilian nobleman and maestre de campo (commander of a tercio), aged 47 at Empel (born December 1537). Greying beard, weathered face. Plain blackened breastplate and gorget over a dark doublet, small ruff, trunk hose, boots, heavy wool cloak, red officer's sash. No full plate, no plumed helmet. Tired and watchful rather than triumphant.
- **Likeness:** no verified likeness exists; the only candidate image was rejected for lack of provenance (see `research.md`). This is an interpretation and is always labelled as one.
- **Documented and interpretation:** his rank, his age and his command of a tercio in the Netherlands in 1585 are documented (`rah-arias-de-bobadilla`). His face, his dress and where he stood at any moment are interpretation.
- **The tercio:** pikemen and arquebusiers of the 1580s in mixed, worn clothing, not a uniform; morions, matchlock guns. Cold, hungry and wet.
- **Hohenlohe's fleet:** shallow river vessels of the Dutch States. Show a few; do not give a countable fleet (the figure is disputed).

## Do not show

- Killing on the ice, or any graphic fighting. The attack on the fleet is given only as what Spanish accounts describe.
- An apparition, a beam of light or any visual that presents the frost as a proven miracle.
- The finding of the panel as fact; it always carries the tradition caption.
- The hanging of the ship's captain in 1588 or the execution of the Justicia of Aragon; neither is narrated here.
- Anachronisms: the red-and-yellow Spanish flag, the later Dutch red-white-blue, 17th-century buff coats and broad lace collars, flintlocks, large ocean-going warships on the river.

## Numbers allowed in voiceover

- Dates only: November 1585; the night of 7 December and the morning of 8 December 1585 (`bossche-encyclopedie-empel`, `army-infanteria-2025`).
- 1892 for the official patronage of the infantry, if used (`army-infanteria-2019`).
- No size for the rebel fleet: ten ships is the Spanish Army's count and is marked disputed.
- No figure for Bobadilla's men: no source in the legend file gives one.
