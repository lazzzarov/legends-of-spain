## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **"First anthropologist".** The RAH entry asserts it ("padre de la moderna antropología cultural"). The dossier and Getty material stress the missionary purpose and the Nahua authorship. Labelled `disputed`, with both sides stated.
- **"Sahagún's book".** Labelled `legend`: the Getty material names Nahua grammarians and scribes and many anonymous artists, and treats Nahuatl as the primary text.
- **Dates of the work.** RAH dates the _Historia general_ 1547–1569 (first complete version); Getty dates the Florentine manuscript 1575–1577. Both are used, for different stages.
- **Death date.** RAH: "several dates are given"; 5 Feb 1590 "most probable". Used as given.
- **Epidemic figures.** Single modern estimates; used only in the otherSide in general terms ("millions").
