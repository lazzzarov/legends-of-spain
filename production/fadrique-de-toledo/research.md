## Disputed points and handling

- **Birth date and place:** see above. The placeholder origin (Naples) is kept, matching both the RAH and BRASILHIS.
- **Fleet size:** given as a range of 52–62 ships and c. 12,000–12,600 men, both marked `disputed`.
- **"Spanish" victory:** reframed throughout as a joint Iberian operation under one king.
- **Prado claim:** I could not open the Prado page (a Cloudflare bot check), and I did not try to bypass it. The statement is attributed to the Prado based on the search-engine extract of that exact page, and the source's `notes` say so. **A human must verify it on museodelprado.es before review.**
