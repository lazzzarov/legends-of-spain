## Disputed points and how they were handled

- **Size of the party.** Carvajal and RAH's Gonzalo Pizarro and Carvajal entries give 57; RAH's Orellana entry gives 56 (and lists names plus two unnamed Black men). The fact shows "56–57" as `disputed`. The prose uses Carvajal's own 57.
- **"Treason".** Shown as `disputed`, with both sides given: Pizarro's accusation against Carvajal's account of the current.
- **Birth year.** Biographers have quoted dates from about 1490 to 1511 (seen in search results, not verified). The file follows RAH with `approx: true`.
- **Date of reaching Cubagua.** 11 or 16 September 1542; the text gives the month only.
- **The Amazons.** Carvajal's report is presented as his claim ("he says"), not as fact.
- **"Discovered the Amazon".** Shown as a myth (`legend`).

## Open questions
